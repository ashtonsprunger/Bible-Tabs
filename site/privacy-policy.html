Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026 · Bible Tabs (ashtonsprunger.bibletabs) · Sprunger Studios

In plain English: Bible Tabs works offline and collects no personal data. We do not track you, show ads, require accounts, or store your notes on any server. Everything you save stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

None. Bible Tabs does not require account registration, does not connect to any Sprunger Studios user server, does not include advertising SDKs or behavioral tracking, and does not collect analytics, crash telemetry, location data, contacts, or device identifiers.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To preserve your reading workspace between sessions, Bible Tabs stores the following information strictly on your device (or in your browser's local storage when using the Web Reader):

  • Open tabs and reading positions: Your open tabs, custom tab names, active tab, and current book, chapter, and verse scroll positions.
  • Highlights and study notes: Verse highlights and personal notes are stored in a local database (user_data_v1.db on Android/iOS, or localStorage on web). You can manually export or import a JSON backup file at any time in Settings.
  • Preferences: Selected Bible translation (BSB, KJV, WEB, or ASV), reading theme (Light, Sepia, or Dark), accent color, text size, and screen orientation.
  • Offline Scripture databases: Public Domain Bible translation files cached locally so reading and search work offline.

On Android, your operating system's built-in Google Backup (Android Auto Backup) may back up your small local user data (tabs, highlights, and notes) to your personal Google account according to your Android system settings. Large read-only Bible databases are excluded from cloud backup. Sprunger Studios has no access to your device backups.

3. Optional External Links

Bible Tabs only opens external apps or websites when you explicitly choose to do so:

  • Book overviews: Long-pressing a book title or tapping Info opens the book overview on GotQuestions.org in your browser.
  • Copy, Share, and Backup Export: Copies text to your clipboard, opens your device's native share sheet, or saves a local JSON file.
  • Store & publisher links: Links in the Attribution screen open Google Play or the corresponding Scripture publisher's website.

4. Deleting Your Data

Because all data is stored locally, you can remove individual tabs, highlights, or notes inside the app, or clear all data immediately from your device or browser settings. See Data & Deletion for step-by-step instructions.

5. Children's Privacy

Bible Tabs does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
