Privacy Policy — Bible Tabs

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 • Developer: Sprunger Studios (Ashton Sprunger)

Sprunger Studios ("we", "us", or "our") built the mobile application Bible Tabs (package ID / bundle ID ashtonsprunger.bibletabs) as a free, ad-free Bible reading app. This page informs users regarding our policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using Bible Tabs.

In plain English: Bible Tabs works 100% offline. We do not collect, track, transmit, or sell any personal data, usage analytics, or reading history. Everything you save in Bible Tabs stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

None. Bible Tabs does not require account registration or login, does not connect to any Sprunger Studios server, does not include third-party advertising networks or behavioral tracking, and does not collect analytics, crash telemetry, location data, contacts, or device identifiers.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To remember your reading workspace between sessions, Bible Tabs stores the following data strictly on your device using local app storage:

  • Open Tabs & Reading Position: Your open Bible tabs, any custom names you assign to tabs, your active tab, and each tab's current book, chapter, and verse scroll position.
  • Display Preferences: Your chosen reading theme (Light, Sepia, or Dark), accent color, text size, and screen orientation preference.
  • Offline Scripture Database: The Berean Standard Bible (BSB) text database is bundled inside the app so reading and full-text search run completely offline without network requests.

Your device's operating system (such as Android Auto Backup or iOS iCloud Backup) may back up local app data according to your personal device backup settings. Sprunger Studios has no access to those backups.

3. Optional External Links & Device Features

Bible Tabs only interacts with external services when you explicitly tap a corresponding action:

  • Book Overviews: Tapping a book overview link opens the relevant page on GotQuestions.org in your device's web browser.
  • Copy & Share Verse: Long-pressing a verse lets you copy the verse text to your local clipboard or send it to another app via your device's native share sheet.
  • App Store Reviews: Tapping the review link in the version/release notes screen opens the official Bible Tabs store listing on Google Play.

Any external website or third-party app you choose to open is governed by its own privacy policy, not this one.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Because Bible Tabs does not collect or transmit user data, we do not share, rent, sell, or disclose any personal information to third parties or data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Sprunger Studios retains 0 bytes of user data on any external server. You can remove individual tabs inside the app at any time, or permanently delete all locally stored tabs and preferences via your device's app storage settings or by uninstalling Bible Tabs. Full step-by-step instructions are available at bibletabs.sprungerstudios.app/delete-data.html.

6. Children's Privacy

Bible Tabs is suitable for all ages and does not collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we introduce optional features in future versions of Bible Tabs that affect how information is stored or transmitted, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly and revise the Effective Date at the top of this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Bible Tabs, please contact Sprunger Studios at support@sprungerstudios.app or via the developer contact listed on the official Bible Tabs app store page.
