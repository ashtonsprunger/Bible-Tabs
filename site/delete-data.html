Data & Deletion

Bible Tabs (ashtonsprunger.bibletabs) · Sprunger Studios

Bible Tabs does not create user accounts or store any data on external servers. All tabs, highlights, study notes, and preferences live strictly on your own device and can be removed immediately at any time.

1. Delete Individual Items Inside Bible Tabs

  • Remove a tab: Tap the active tab in the top bar, then tap Remove Tab.
  • Remove a highlight or note: Tap or long-press the verse to clear a highlight or delete a note, or manage items from the Notebook screen.

2. Delete All Local Data Permanently

On Android: Open system Settings → Apps → Bible Tabs → Storage & cache → Clear storage (or uninstall the app).

On iOS: Press and hold the Bible Tabs icon and choose Remove App → Delete App.

In the Web Reader: Clear site data / cookies for bibletabs.sprungerstudios.app in your browser settings.

3. Retention Summary

Deleted locally Open tabs, custom tab names, reading positions, verse highlights, study notes, and display/translation preferences.
Kept on servers None (0 bytes). Sprunger Studios never receives or stores your data.
Retention period None — deletion takes effect immediately on your device.
