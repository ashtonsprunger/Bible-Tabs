Data & Deletion — Bible Tabs

App: Bible Tabs (ashtonsprunger.bibletabs) • Developer: Sprunger Studios (Ashton Sprunger)

Bible Tabs by Sprunger Studios operates 100% offline. It does not create user accounts, does not collect personal profiles, and does not sync or store any user data on Sprunger Studios servers. All open tabs and display settings reside strictly on your own device and can be deleted immediately at any time using the steps below.

1. Remove Individual Tabs Inside Bible Tabs

  1. Open Bible Tabs on your device.
  2. Tap the currently active tab in the top tab bar to open the Tab menu.
  3. Tap Remove Tab to delete that tab and its saved scroll position immediately.

2. Permanently Delete All Local App Data

On Android:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps → Bible Tabs → Storage & cache.
  3. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data) to erase all saved tabs and preferences, or simply uninstall Bible Tabs.

On iOS:

  1. Press and hold the Bible Tabs icon on your Home Screen and choose Remove App → Delete App to permanently remove all local app data.

3. Types of Data Deleted, Kept & Retention Period

  • Data Deleted on Your Device:
    • Saved Bible tabs, custom tab names, active tab index, and chapter/verse reading positions.
    • Local appearance preferences (Light/Sepia/Dark theme, accent color, text size, and screen orientation setting).
  • Data Kept by Sprunger Studios: None (0 bytes). Bible Tabs never uploads or stores user data on external servers.
  • Additional Retention Period: None (immediate local deletion).
