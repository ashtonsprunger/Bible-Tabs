Key Features
☰

Browser-Style Multi-Tabs

Open as many tabs as you need, give any tab a custom study label, and flip between passages instantly while each tab keeps its place.

¶

Print-Style Typesetting

Typeset directly from Berean Standard Bible (BSB) USJ formatting with flowing paragraphs, indented Hebrew poetry, headings, Psalm titles, Selah, and footnotes.

↕

Continuous Whole-Bible Scroll

Scroll seamlessly through chapters and straight across book boundaries without interruption, with live tab labels syncing as you read.

⌕

Instant Live Search & Jump

Type keywords for live full-text verse matches or enter a reference like Psalm 23 or Rom 8:28 to jump directly—100% offline.

❝

Long-Press Verse Actions

Tap and hold any verse to copy formatted citations to your clipboard, share through your device's share sheet, or open a book overview.

Choose Light, Sepia, or Dark reading themes, 8 accent colors, 5 comfortable text sizes, and Portrait or Auto-Rotate screen orientation.
