Read Scripture with browser-style tabs.

Bible Tabs is a free, quiet Bible reader built for cross-referencing and daily study. Keep multiple passages open in separate tabs, highlight verses, write local notes, and read without ads, accounts, or clutter.

Open Web Reader → Get on Google Play

Designed to stay out of the way

  • Multi-tab reading. Open as many tabs as you need for a sermon, devotional, or cross-reference. Rename or reorder tabs anytime; every tab remembers its exact verse.
  • Print-style typesetting. Clean chapter-by-chapter reading with flowing prose paragraphs, indented Hebrew poetry, section headings, Psalm titles, and translator footnotes.
  • Multi-verse highlights and notes. Select one verse or a whole range to highlight in six theme-tuned colors, attach private study notes, and review everything in your Notebook.
  • Fast search and reference jump. Search full Scripture text or jump straight to a passage like Psalm 23 or Rom 8:28.
  • 7 Bible translations. Read four bundled translations completely offline (BSB, KJV, WEB, ASV), plus online NIV, NLT, and NASB 1995 via API.Bible.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your tabs, highlights, and notes stay on your device, with built-in JSON backup export and import whenever you switch devices.

Available on Android and the Web

Use the Web Reader right in your browser with no sign-in required. On Android, install Bible Tabs on Google Play.
