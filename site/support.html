Bible Tabs (ashtonsprunger.bibletabs) · Android & Web

Quick Tips

  • Manage tabs: Tap + to open a new tab. Tap the currently active tab to rename it, pick a book and chapter, open your Notebook, search, or open Settings.
  • Highlight & take notes: On mobile, press and hold any verse, then tap additional verses to select a range. On desktop web, simply click verses to select them. Pick a highlight color or tap + Note.
  • Notebook: Tap the active tab, then tap the Notebook icon in the top right to browse, search, filter by highlight color, or open saved passages in a new tab.
  • Fast search: Type keywords to search every verse offline, or type a reference like Psalm 23 or John 3:16 to jump directly. On desktop web, press Ctrl+K or /.
  • Move highlights & notes between devices: In Settings, tap Backup → Export to save or copy a JSON backup, then tap Import on your other device to merge everything cleanly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Bible Tabs work without an internet connection?

Yes. On Android, all four bundled translations (BSB, KJV, WEB, ASV) and full-text search are stored inside the app and work 100% offline. In the Web Reader, each translation caches locally in your browser the first time you open it.

Which translations are included?

Bible Tabs currently includes four complete Public Domain translations: Berean Standard Bible (BSB), King James Version (KJV), World English Bible (WEB), and American Standard Version (ASV, 1901). Switch translations globally anytime in Settings—your tabs, highlights, and notes automatically stay in place.

Will my highlights and notes be backed up if I get a new Android phone?

Yes. Bible Tabs stores highlights and notes in a dedicated lightweight database that is included in Android's built-in Google Backup (while excluding large Bible files so backups remain small and reliable). For extra peace of mind—or moving data between Android and the Web Reader—you can also export and import a .json backup anytime in Settings.

Does Bible Tabs have ads, subscriptions, or accounts?

No. Bible Tabs is completely free, ad-free, requires no sign-in, and collects no personal data.
