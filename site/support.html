Support & FAQ — Bible Tabs

App: Bible Tabs (v5.1.0 • ashtonsprunger.bibletabs) • Developer: Sprunger Studios

Quick Tips

  • Open & switch tabs: Tap + in the top tab bar to open a new Bible tab, or tap any inactive tab to jump straight to where you left off.
  • Change book, search, rename, or open settings: Tap the active tab to open the Tab menu—rename your tab, pick any book and chapter, tap the search icon, or open Settings (⚙).
  • Quick reference jump: In Search, type keywords to search every verse offline, or type a passage like Psalm 23 or John 3:16 for an instant jump card.
  • Copy or share a verse: Press and hold any verse to open the quick action bar for copying with citation or sharing.
  • Book overviews: Tap a book title banner to open a study overview of that book.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Bible Tabs work without an internet connection?

Yes. The entire 66-book Berean Standard Bible (BSB) and full-text search index are bundled locally inside the app, so reading, tabbing, and searching work 100% offline.

Which Bible translation does Bible Tabs use?

Bible Tabs (v5.1.0) uses the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), typeset directly from its official USJ formatting with flowing prose paragraphs, indented poetry, section headings, cross-references, Psalm superscriptions, Selah, and translator footnotes.

Does Bible Tabs have ads, subscriptions, or user accounts?

No. Bible Tabs is completely free, has zero ads, requires no sign-in, and collects zero personal data. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I change the theme, accent color, text size, or screen rotation?

Tap the active tab in the top bar, then tap the gear icon (⚙) in the top right to open Settings. From there you can switch between Light, Sepia, and Dark themes, choose from 8 accent colors, pick from 5 text sizes, and toggle Portrait vs. Any orientation.
