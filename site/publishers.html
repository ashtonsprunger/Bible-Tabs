Publisher & Licensing Information

For Scripture rights holders and permissions teams

Bible Tabs is a small, independent, completely free Bible reader developed by Ashton Sprunger (Sprunger Studios). Readers open browser-style tabs for different passages, read print-typeset Scripture, highlight verses, and keep private local study notes.

Below are transparent details about the app, its modest audience size, and how we handle copyright compliance and text protection for any licensed translation.

Commitments to Scripture Publishers

  1. Strictly non-commercial. Bible Tabs generates zero revenue and contains no advertisements or commercial upsells.
  2. No AI or machine learning use. Scripture text is presented solely for human reading and personal study. It is never sent to language models, embedded, or used for training.
  3. Exact text integrity. We display unmodified text from publisher-approved source files or APIs, faithfully preserving paragraphs, poetry, headings, and footnotes.
  4. Clear copyright attribution. Every translation includes its exact required copyright notice, registered trademarks, and a clickable link to the publisher's official website in Settings, our Translations & Attribution screen, and shared verse citations.
  5. Protected source handling & flexible delivery. Licensed Bible files are never committed to public source repositories. While Bible Tabs is designed around offline reading, we gladly follow each publisher's preferred terms—whether protected offline packaging, on-demand downloads, or authorized API integration (such as the ESV API or API.Bible with FUMS tracking).
  6. Transparent compliance. We gladly observe any active-user caps, provide periodic usage updates upon request, and update or remove a translation promptly if terms change.
